TEAM ROCKET TERROR: MYTHICAL POKÉMON EXTRACTION THREATENS UNOVA

ABYSSAL RUINS CRISIS: GIOVANNI'S ENERGY MACHINE DEFEATED BY LOCAL HEROES

UNDISCLOSED LOCATION (UNOVA) : In a shocking escalation of criminal activity, the notorious syndicates known as Team Rocket launched a massive assault on the Unova region's coast, briefly seizing control of the ancient Abyssal Ruins. Eyewitnesses report that the group's leader, Giovanni, spearheaded an operation utilizing highly advanced, coercive technology to trap the rare, Mythical Pokémon Meloetta inside an energy-extraction chamber. The machine violently harvested Meloetta's mystical songs to activate the legendary Reveal Glass, a relic capable of summoning and controlling the volatile Forces of Nature—Tornadus, Thundurus, and Landorus. The resulting atmospheric instability triggered severe weather anomalies across the region, nearly devastating the local ecosystem. Fortunately, a brave intervention by local trainers and the Unova Champion disrupted the energy siphon, destroying Team Rocket's extraction machinery and forcing a full tactical retreat by the criminal organization. Meloetta is currently reported to be safe and recovering in a secluded sanctuary.
