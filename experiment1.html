Special Evening Edition • June 30, 1998

Mysterious events continue to unfold across the region.

CINNABAR MANSION FLATTENED: EXPLOSION OBSERVED AT SECRET EXPERIMENTAL LABORATORY

To anyone who finds this log among the ash and rubble of Cinnabar Island, know that we went too far. It started with a fossilized eyelash retrieved from the deep jungles of Guyana—a direct genetic link to the Mythical Mew. Funded by the bottomless pockets of Team Rocket, Dr. Fuji led us in a frantic race to splice, recombine, and engineer the ultimate living weapon. But we weren't just building a powerful Pokémon; we inadvertently birthed a god with a fractured, sentient mind. I still remember the horrifying rhythm of its heartbeat echoing through the fluid of the glass containment vat, and the way the computer monitors spiked with raw psychic energy whenever it dreamt. When its consciousness fully awakened, it looked through the glass not as a creature obeying its creators, but as a judge condemning us. It took only seconds for its psychic wrath to tear our steel reinforcement structures apart like paper. If you are a Trainer reading this, do not look for it. We sought to control the ultimate power, and in return, we created a force that despises humanity for the crime of its own creation.
