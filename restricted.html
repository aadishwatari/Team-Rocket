Internal Rocket Archives — Level 7 Clearance Required.

This document was recovered from an abandoned research facility beneath the Viridian sector. Most records suffered extensive damage during the incident of ████████.

Intelligence reports indicate that Project Eclipse was initiated under direct authorization from ████████████. The objective involved the tracking and containment of unusually powerful Pokémon displaying abnormal behavioral patterns.

Field agents recovered several artifacts from Site-03, including encrypted transmissions and references to a location known only as "The Hollow Chamber."

Witness testimony remains inconsistent. Multiple personnel reported hearing mechanical sounds originating from sealed sections of the complex despite the facility being officially abandoned for over ███ years.

Analysis suggests the message was intended for high-ranking Rocket executives and may reference a dormant experiment designated E-17.

Personnel assigned to Project Eclipse included researchers from the Celadon and Cinnabar divisions. Most identities have since been removed from public records.
